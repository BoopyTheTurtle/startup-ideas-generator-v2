# Map the work

Input: a vertical and a country (default country: France). Output:
`verticals/<vertical>-<cc>/workflows.md`.

## Procedure

1. Find documents that already list what the profession does. Search in the country's
   language. Good ones: statutory or customary scopes of service and fee scales (for
   French architects, the *missions* of the loi MOP: ESQ, APS, APD, PRO, ACT, VISA, DET,
   AOR), chamber or order guidance, accreditation standards, standard contracts, job
   descriptions.
2. List 10 to 15 core workflows. For each: who does it (role, firm type), what it
   produces, who receives it (client, authority, contractor), and which rules govern it.
3. Include the firms around the profession where work crosses the boundary (for
   architecture: engineering offices, permit authorities, contractors, developers).
4. Note for each workflow who might measure its failure: an authority, a regulator, an
   insurer, a chamber, a court, a statistics office. Step 2 starts from this column.

## Gate

Keep core work only: how the profession's product gets made, approved, and delivered.
Drop marketing, generic administration, and anything a thin wrapper or browser extension
would fix, unless it recurs across most firms in the vertical; mark those `recurring`.

## Rules

- Cite a source for each workflow. Mark each citation `[P]` (primary source read) or
  `[S]` (search summary or secondary report).
- Assume nothing about the team's skills, capital, or contacts.
