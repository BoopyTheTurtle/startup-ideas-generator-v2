# Architecture, France: core workflows

Step 1 output, 2026-11-16. Marks: **[P]** primary source read, **[S]** search summary or
secondary source.

## The profession in numbers

- 29,521 architects were registered with the Order in 2023, a figure stable near 30,000
  since 2010. [S] (CNOA, *Archigraphie 2024-2026*)
- 54% of agencies are one person, 35% have 2 to 5 people, 11% have 6 or more. [P]
- 98% of agencies do design studies and 91% supervise sites. [P]
- 95% work on existing buildings; for 48% of agencies that work exceeds 75% of revenue.
  [P]
- 62% of agencies took public contracts in 2024; for 26% they exceed half of revenue. [P]
- Half of agencies use AI, mostly to write text (79% of users) and make images (37%);
  10% use it in design. [P]

The [P] figures come from the CREDOC barometer of agencies, 2,434 respondents, fieldwork
September 17 to October 17, 2025.

## Workflows

The standard *missions de maîtrise d'œuvre* (Code de la commande publique, art. R.
2431-4 and following; formerly loi MOP) give the backbone [S]. Rows 1, 5, 12, and 13 sit
outside the standard missions but are core to practice.

| No. | Workflow | Who does it | Produces | Receives it | Who might measure failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Survey and diagnosis of an existing building (relevé, DIAG) | Architect, surveyor, diagnosticians | Measured drawings, condition report | Client, design team | AQC (renovation defects), insurers |
| 2 | Sketch and preliminary design (ESQ, APS, APD) | Architect with engineering offices | Plans, cost estimate, regulatory notices | Client | Chambers (cost overruns), courts |
| 3 | Thermal and environmental study (RE2020: energy, carbon, summer comfort) | Thermal engineering office, with the architect's inputs | Calculation, standardised summary, attestations at permit and at completion | Permit authority, state inspectors | State inspections (CRC), Cerema |
| 4 | Building permit file (permis de construire) | Architect (mandatory above 150 m²) | Forms, plans, notices, attestations | Municipal permit service | Permit services, Sitadel statistics, administrative courts |
| 5 | Regulatory compliance of the design: accessibility, fire safety, acoustics, ventilation, guard rails | Architect, technical inspector (contrôleur technique) | Compliant plans, attestations at completion | Client, state inspectors | State inspections (CRC), regional DREAL reports |
| 6 | Detailed design and specifications (PRO; CCTP, quantities) | Architect, economist, engineering offices | Tender documents (DCE) | Contractors | Insurers (claims traced to design), courts |
| 7 | Contractor tender and award (ACT) | Architect, client | Bid analysis, contracts | Client | Public buyers, courts |
| 8 | Review of contractors' execution drawings (VISA) | Architect, engineering offices | Stamped drawings, comments | Contractors | Insurers, AQC |
| 9 | Site supervision (DET): meetings, minutes, checking work, certifying payments | Architect | Site minutes, payment certificates | Client, contractors | Insurers (MAF), AQC Sycodés, courts |
| 10 | Scheduling and coordination (OPC) | Architect or specialist | Schedule, coordination minutes | Client, contractors | Developers (delay), regional state reports |
| 11 | Handover and defects period (AOR): snag lists, lifting reserves, as-built file | Architect | Reception report, reserve list, DOE | Client | Consumer bodies, social landlords, insurers |
| 12 | Winning work: public tenders and competitions | Architect | Applications, bids, competition entries | Public buyers | Ministry of Culture studies, MIQCP, CNOA |
| 13 | Insurance and liability: yearly declaration of works to the insurer, claims handling | Architect | Declaration of activity, claim files | Insurer (MAF covers most architects) | MAF annual report |

## Dropped at the gate

Agency marketing, invoicing, time tracking, and recruitment: generic administration. Fee
collection is kept under watch as `recurring` if a measure turns up; one search found
none.
